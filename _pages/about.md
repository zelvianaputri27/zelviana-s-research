## About Me

I am an M.Sc. graduate in Advanced Bioconvergence with interdisciplinary research experience integrating metabolomics, bioinformatics, analytical chemistry, and cellular biology to identify bioactive compounds and investigate their molecular mechanisms relevant to human health.

My research experience spans both experimental and computational approaches, including natural product isolation and characterization, chromatographic and spectroscopic techniques, molecular docking, network pharmacology, and biological assays. Through these approaches, I have developed an interest in understanding how molecular-level changes translate into biological functions and potential health outcomes.

My long-term goal is to become a **translational biomedical researcher**, integrating omics technologies, computational biology, and biotechnology to advance therapeutic discovery and improve our understanding of human disease.

## Research Journey

My academic journey began with a background in food science and natural products and has progressively expanded toward biomedical and computational research.

During my Master's research, I investigated bioactive compounds from natural products by combining analytical and computational approaches with biological validation. This experience introduced me to the integration of metabolomics, network pharmacology, molecular docking, and *Caenorhabditis elegans* assays to investigate potential biological mechanisms.

I am now expanding my expertise toward **multi-omics and computational biology**, with a particular interest in bulk and single-cell transcriptomics, spatial omics, functional genomics, and computational drug discovery.

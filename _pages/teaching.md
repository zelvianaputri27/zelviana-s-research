## Teaching Experiences

During my undergraduate studies at Brawijaya University, I worked as a laboratory assistant for several undergraduate laboratory courses. I supported lecturers in preparing practical sessions and assisted students during laboratory experiments.

I worked with more than **200 undergraduate students** across different laboratory courses.

---

## Laboratory Assistant — Nutrition and Food Evaluation

**Brawijaya University, Malang, Indonesia**

- Assisted lecturers in preparing and conducting laboratory sessions.
- Guided undergraduate students during practical experiments.
- Helped prepare laboratory materials, equipment, and experimental procedures.
- Assisted students with basic laboratory techniques and experimental procedures.

**Laboratory topics included:**

- Starch nutritional value
- Dietary fiber
- Protein nutritional value
- Tannin and phytate determination
- Experimental animal procedures

## Laboratory Assistant — Biochemistry and Food Analysis

**Brawijaya University, Malang, Indonesia**

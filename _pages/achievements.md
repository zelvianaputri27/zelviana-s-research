## Notable Achievements

### 🥇 Best Research Poster
**Korean Society of Food Science and Technology (KoSFoST) — 2025 | South Korea**

Recognized with the **Best Research Poster** award for research on the isolation, structural elucidation, and molecular mechanisms underlying the anti-aging effects of xanthohumol using targeted metabolomics, network pharmacology, and *in vitro* studies.

---

### 🔬 Research Grant Recipient
**BK21 Research Graduate Competition — 2025 | South Korea**

Selected as a recipient of a research grant through the Brain Korea 21 (BK21) Research Graduate Competition for the proposed project:

*Integrated Upcycling of Pineapple Peel Waste through Isolation of Bioactive Natural Compounds and Biochar Production.*

---

### 🥇 Gold Medal
**Korea International Women's Invention & Exposition — 2019 | South Korea**

Received a Gold Medal for the proposed innovation:

*Analog Rice from Tapioca and Sorghum Flour with Fortification of Moringa Leaves Flour to Combat Malnutrition in Realizing SDGs.*

---

### 🥇 1st Winner
**National Food Technology Competition — 2019 | Indonesia**

Awarded 1st place for the proposed innovation:

*Innovation of Synbiotic Yogurt Jelly Drink Made from Local-Based Food to Combat Colorectal Cancer (CRC) in Indonesia.*

---

### 🌏 International Competition Participant
**Institute of Food Technologists Student Association Competition — 2019 | USA**

Participated in an international food innovation competition with the proposed product:

*Instant Sweet Porridge to Combat Vitamin A Deficiency of Kenyan Preschool Children using Baby Corn, Baby Carrot, and Banana Peels.*
